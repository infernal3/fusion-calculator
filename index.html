There are three different variants of Attribute Fusion: ID Fusion, Special Fusion, and Chameleon.
ID Fusion uses the ID, rarity and attribute category of one shard to determine the output shard.
Special Fusion uses predefined special fusion recipes to determine an output shard. Special Fusions grant 2 shards when fused.
Chameleon Fusion takes the ID of the non-chameleon shard and outputs the next 3 IDs sequentially.
